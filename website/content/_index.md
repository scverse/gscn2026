This workshop introduces stem cell biologists to computational single-cell genomics,
analysing data with software from the [scverse](https://scverse.org) ecosystem. Over one
and a half days we work through two modalities hands-on: single-cell RNA sequencing and
spatial transcriptomics.

Each block pairs a short lecture with a longer coding session. Participants work in two
groups on the same material at different depths:

- **Beginners**: first steps into single-cell data, little to no coding experience.
- **Advanced**: already working with data, and some coding experience, ideally in Python.

### What you will take away

- How single-cell data analysis works, from raw counts to annotated cell types.
- How to preprocess, quality-control, cluster and visualise the data with `scanpy` and
  `squidpy`.
- Hands-on experience with trajectory inference, batch-effect correction, cell-cell
  communication and spatial analysis.

**Bring your own laptop.** Everything runs locally - see the [setup instructions](setup/).
Day 1 opens with a dedicated setup hour, from 12:00 to 13:00, where we get everyone's
environment working together.

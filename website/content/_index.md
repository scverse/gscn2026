This is the fourth edition of the scverse x GSCN workshop on computational single-cell
genomics. Over one and a half days we work through a full analysis, from raw counts to
spatial context, using the [scverse](https://scverse.org) ecosystem: `scanpy`, `anndata`,
`squidpy` and `spatialdata`.

Each block pairs a short common lecture with a longer hands-on session. Participants work
in two groups, **beginner** and **advanced**, on the same topics at different depths, with
instructors circulating in both rooms.

**Bring your own laptop.** Everything runs locally, so please install the software before
you arrive - see [Before you arrive](#setup) below. There is a setup and troubleshooting
session at 11:00 on day 1 for anything that did not work.
